# How tdJamaat writes

A short guide for anyone adding text to the app, in Kyrgyz or English.
Kyrgyz is written first; English follows the meaning, not the words.

## Who we talk to

- **Kyrgyz: «сиз».** Polite and warm, never stiff. «Кириңиз», not
  «Сураныч, кирүүнү аткарыңыз».
- **English: "you".** Plain and direct. "Sign in with Google", not
  "Please authenticate using your Google account".
- A button says what happens: «Сактоо», «Кирүү», «Жаңы сөз». It does not
  say «Бул жерди басыңыз».

## Tone

- Say what is true and what to do next. «Капитаныңыз Gmail дарегиңизди
  жаза элек болушу мүмкүн. Ага айтыңыз.»
- No cheering. No "Awesome!", «Азаматсыз!», exclamation marks or emoji in
  the interface. The numbers speak for themselves.
- No motivational filler: no "unlock your potential", «жаңы бийиктиктерге».
- Progress is a fact, not a judgement: «3 / 5 үй киргизди», «планга
  жетүү үчүн жумасына ~10 аят».
- A low week is said gently, once: «Кийинки аптада көтөрөбүз,
  иншаллах.» Don't repeat it in three places.
- Errors say what went wrong in human terms first, and the technical
  message after it, only if it helps the admin.

## Form

- Short sentences. One idea in each.
- No long dashes in running text. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Numbers as digits: «7 бет», "7 pages".
- Dates: «26-ноябрь» / "26 November". Weeks: «19–25 ноябрь».
- Kyrgyz quotation marks «…», English straight "…" in the code.
- Headings are a few words, no full stop. Hints under them are full
  sentences.

## What we never rewrite

Quran text, translations of its meaning, hadith wording, names of
supplications and religious terms are copied from their sources and left
exactly as they are. Style rules don't apply to them.
